Pass Tiers, Inclusions & Concessions +

The 808 Clash Pass (€10.00): Grants full backstage producer warmup studio access, official sample pack USB cassette, and stage battle entry for the afternoon tournament.

DJ WARP Concert Pass (€25.00): Grants floor admission to the headline evening concert in Underground Vault 02, including access to the quad-turntable audio masterclass.

All-Access Bundle (€30.00): Combines both passes with an automatic €5.00 bundle discount. FBAUP students presenting valid UP student ID at the door receive a complimentary festival wristband.

Acoustic Engineering in Stone Vaults +

Underground Vault 02 features 19th-century granite masonry. A custom 4-point sound reinforcement system with cardioid subwoofer arrays has been tuned specifically to avoid standing bass waves, providing punchy kick transients and crystal-clear high-frequency needle scratch definition throughout the stone arches.

Directions, Transit & Accessibility +

Address: Edifício da Alfândega, Rua Nova da Alfândega, 4050-430 Porto.

Public Transit: Historic Tram 1 stops right outside the main gate. STCP Bus lines 500, 403, and 1M drop off at "Alfândega". The venue is fully wheelchair accessible with ramped access into Vault 02.
