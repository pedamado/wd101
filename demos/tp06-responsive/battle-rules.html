THE 808 CLASH: OFFICIAL BATTLE RULES

Underground Vault 02 transforms into an electrified sonic battle ring. Sixteen producers face off over hardware samplers and twin turntables. No presets, no pre-rendered laptop stems—pure spontaneous groove warfare judged by Grand Master DJ WARP.

1. TOURNAMENT BRACKET & FORMAT

PHASE 01

Round of 16: The Crate Flip (60 Seconds)

Each competitor plays one 60-second original beat chopped from an assigned 1970s jazz/funk vinyl sample provided 24 hours prior. Coin toss decides who drops first.

PHASE 02

Quarterfinals: 808 Sub Warfare (Two 45s Rounds)

Producers exchange two consecutive 45-second rounds. Focus is strictly on low-end weight, sub-bass tuning, and drum groove dynamics through the vault sound system.

PHASE 03

Semifinals: Live Finger Drumming Sprint (90 Seconds)

No sequencer playback allowed. Beats must be tapped live on rubber pads in real time. Tempo must stay between 86 and 96 BPM.

GRAND FINAL

The Mystery Acetate Blind Flip (3 Rounds)

Both finalists receive an unlabelled vinyl dubplate selected on the spot by DJ WARP. They get 3 minutes on stage to sample, chop, and play their live routine.

2. HARDWARE & GEAR REGULATIONS

STRICT HARDWARE ENFORCEMENT

To preserve analog immediacy, NO LAPTOPS, TABLETS, OR PRE-RENDERED DIGITAL AUDIO WORKSTATIONS (DAWs) are allowed on the competition stage.

  • Permitted Samplers: Akai MPC (all standalone models), Roland SP-404 / SP-303, Native Instruments Maschine+ (standalone mode), Elektron Octatrack / Digitakt.
  • Audio Outputs: Two balanced 1/4" TRS or RCA lines directly to the main battle mixer.
  • Pre-Loaded Sounds: Drums and sound clips may be pre-loaded onto SD cards or flash memory; however, all arrangement and trigger patterns must be triggered or modulated live.
OFFICIAL EVALUATION CRITERIA

JUDGING RUBRIC (100 PTS TOTAL)

Judged by Grand Master DJ WARP alongside two guest veteran producers from the Porto underground circuit.

Swing, Pocket & Human Groove 25 PTS

Micro-timing, unquantized shuffle, snare pocket, and head-nod factor.

Sound Design & 808 Sub Control 25 PTS

Clean low-end harmonic saturation, kick punch, and kick/bass phase alignment.

Sample Chopping & Harmonic Cleverness 25 PTS

Creative re-contextualization of source material, melodic flips, and pitch manipulation.

Stage Energy & Decibel Meter Response 25 PTS

Crowd uproar, performance flair, pad attack technique, and battle presence.
