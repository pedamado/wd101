INFORMATION & ACCESS

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access Alfândega do Porto via Public Transit?
Historic Tram Line 1 stops directly in front of the Alfândega riverfront terminal. STCP Bus 500 connects directly from São Bento Railway Station and Campanhã Intermodal Hub every 15 minutes.
What are the acoustic conditions of Vault 02?
Vault 02 is a 19th-century subterranean granite warehouse. The room features high-performance bass trapping and directional line-array acoustic dispersion tuned specifically for vinyl turntablism and sub-bass impact.
Is this website built strictly without JavaScript?
Yes! All interactions—including the color scheme theme switch, the X-Ray diagnostic matrix, the 3D vinyl fan-out, the accordion disclosures, and the reactive pricing calculations—are rendered via pure CSS3/4/5 and HTML5 semantic living standards.
